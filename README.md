EditBright is a free AI image editor that runs in the browser. Open the site, upload a photo, describe the change or remove the background, and download the result. You do not need to create an account or log in to use the core tools.

This public repository is the project page for EditBright: product documentation, official links, and brand assets. The live editor is at [editbright.com](https://editbright.com/). This repo is **not** a mirror of the private production codebase and should not be used to change the live site.

## How to edit a photo

1. Open [https://editbright.com/](https://editbright.com/).
2. Upload a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image, or skip the upload to generate from text.
3. Describe what should change, or use one of the on-page examples (such as replace background, change hair color, or a watercolor style).
4. Generate a preview and download the result to your device.

Nothing has to be installed. There is no email form in the way of the free tools.

## Free to use, with an optional paid path

Public copy on the live homepage is the source of truth here. Do not invent user counts, ratings, or prices.

- The core tools are free. The site describes a short wait between edits and a daily limit per visitor.
- The prompt editor and the background remover can be used without creating an account.
- Sign-in is asked only if you want the extra (PRO) models. When it is asked, the site uses a Google sign-in button.
- Extra models are tied to an account with credits, sold in one-time packs with no subscription. See [Pricing](https://editbright.com/pricing) for current packs and terms rather than copying amounts into this README.
- Background removal and photo enhancement are described on the product pages as free to use with no account required, with results in about 20 seconds.

## Files and privacy

From the public FAQ on the homepage:

- Supported files: JPEG, PNG, and WebP, up to 20 MB, between 64 and 8192 pixels on each side.
- Your image is sent to EditBright's AI model providers to process the request you made.
- EditBright does not ask for your name or email to use the free tools.
- Read the full [privacy policy](https://editbright.com/privacy-policy) for details.

## What kinds of edits work well

Short, specific prompts work best. The public FAQ mentions changing backgrounds, adjusting light and color, swapping or removing objects, and changing details such as clothing color.
